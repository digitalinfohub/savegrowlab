The full toolbox

Every calculator, one page.

Seven free tools for planning money in pounds. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. Open a tool, plug in your numbers, then read the linked guide if you want the deeper explanation.

Which one do I need?

Pick by question, not by tool name.

The fastest way to choose: find the question you actually came here with.

“How much per month until my goal?”

Use in Target → monthly mode. If your horizon is three years or more, add an interest rate — it makes the monthly figure noticeably smaller. Then read How to save money consistently.

“Will my money actually grow?”

Use , run it once on compound and once on simple, and compare the gap. That gap is the core argument of our compound interest guide.

“Where does my salary go?”

Start with . If essentials exceed 50–60% of income, the fix is structural (housing, debt) rather than willpower — our saving guide covers the levers.

“Can I afford this holiday?”

Run with a 10–15% buffer, then feed the total into Savings Goal as a target with a deadline.

“Am I prepared for disaster?”
