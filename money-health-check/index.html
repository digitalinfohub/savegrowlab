Money health check

How healthy is your money, really?

Six questions. One score. No sign-up and no judgement. At the end you get the one thing worth fixing first, with the tool that does it.

The guide behind the tool

What the score means

The check scores six habits: an emergency buffer, automatic saving, knowing your essential costs, high-rate debt, bill reviews, and the ability to absorb a £500 shock. Each answer contributes up to three points, giving a total out of 18 which is scaled to 100.

It is not a credit score, and it is not a judgement. A low score usually means one structural thing is missing — usually automation — not that you are bad with money.

The four bands

ScoreBandBest next move
80–100StrongMake the money work harder: compare ISAs and check investment costs.
55–79Solid, with gapsAutomate one thing this week — a standing order is the highest-leverage fix.
30–54FragileBuild a one-month buffer, then attack the most expensive debt.
0–29Needs attentionStart absurdly small: £20 this week, saved somewhere separate.

Why automation beats motivation

Every study of saving behaviour says the same thing: the transfer that happens without a decision beats the intention that requires one. If your score has one weak spot, make it automatic before you optimise anything else. The payday routine guide walks through the exact setup, and the budget planner works out the amount.

Retake it in three months. The score should move because your systems changed, not because your feelings about money did.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a credit check?

No. Nothing leaves your browser and no third party is contacted. It is a self-assessment of habits.

Why only six questions?

Because a longer quiz gets abandoned. Six questions cover the habits that predict resilience better than any others.

What if I score 100?

Then your basics are done. The next chapter is making the money efficient — see the compound calculator and the ISA comparison.

Is my score saved?

The latest score is stored in your browser only, so you can see your progress. Clear site data to remove it.
