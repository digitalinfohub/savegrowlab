Penny · money-saving assistant

Ask Penny. Get a plan, not a lecture.

Penny asks what you are saving for, how much it costs and when you need it — then gives you the monthly amount and the tool to refine it. Everything runs on your device.

✓ Asks the questions✓ Saves your plan✓ No sign-up

How Penny works

Penny is a scripted assistant, not a chatbot trained on your data. It recognises common money questions, runs a short guided flow, and uses the same arithmetic as our calculators. That means the answer you get is the answer the tool would give — and nothing you type is sent to a server.

  • It asks first. Three questions about your goal, the amount and the deadline.
  • It gives a number. The monthly saving needed, plus what your current pace would take.
  • It remembers locally. Save the plan and it appears on the home page and in the calculator on this device.
  • It links the next step. A guide for the habit, a tool for the maths, or both.

Good things to ask

“I want £2,000 for a holiday next summer” “Where should my £2,300 take-home pay go?” “How big should my emergency fund be?” “How long will my credit card take to clear?” “What are my subscriptions costing me?” “How healthy is my money?”

What Penny cannot do

Penny cannot give personalised financial advice, access your bank, or recommend a specific product. It is an educational tool. For debt or benefit problems, contact a free service such as StepChange or MoneyHelper.
