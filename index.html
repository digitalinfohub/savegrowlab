Questions

Quick answers

Is SaveGrow Lab really free?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free, with no account and no premium tier. The site is funded by advertising served by Adsterra.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Calculator inputs are never uploaded, and your saved plan, streak and quiz score live in your browser’s local storage. See the privacy policy.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes — that is what the QR code above is for. Add the site to your home screen and it opens full-screen, like an app, and keeps working offline once loaded.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is general education and planning arithmetic. For advice about your circumstances, speak to a regulated adviser; for free debt help, contact StepChange, National Debtline or MoneyHelper.

Does Penny use artificial intelligence?

Penny is a scripted on-device assistant: it matches your question against rules and runs the same calculators as the rest of the site. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

How often are the guides updated?

Guides are dated and reviewed at least twice a year, and whenever a rate or rule changes materially.

Start with one number.

A savings target, a monthly budget or a subscription total. Any one of them is enough to begin.
