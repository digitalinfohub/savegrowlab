Make the easy money decisions easier.

Small systems can reduce friction without turning every purchase into a spreadsheet.

01 · Pause

Use a 24-hour rule

For non-essential purchases, wait a day before deciding. You can still buy it, but the decision becomes more deliberate.

Good for impulse buys
02 · Automate

Move savings after payday

Choose a manageable amount and move it into savings early, rather than waiting to see what is left.

Consistency beats intensity
03 · Total it up

Watch the basket, not one item

Several “small” purchases can become a large total. A quick estimate keeps the whole basket visible.

Use the £5 rule
04 · Recurring

Audit subscriptions

Review recurring payments regularly and label each as essential, useful or unused before the next renewal.

Low effort
05 · Goals

Name the reason

“£600 for Japan” is more actionable than “save more”. Give the money a target and track the progress.

Make it measurable
06 · Friction

Make expensive habits harder

Remove saved payment details or unnecessary shopping shortcuts when you want a little more time to think.

Understand the basics before chasing returns.

Learn how common investment options work, what risk means and why time and diversification matter.

Cash

Savings accounts

Useful for short-term goals and emergency funds. Interest can provide a return without daily market price movements.

Lower volatility
Funds

Funds & ETFs

A fund can hold many investments, which can make diversification easier. Fees and what the fund owns still matter.

Diversification
Shares

Individual companies

Owning one company gives direct exposure to its performance, which can mean bigger company-specific swings.

Higher concentration
Debt

Bonds

Bonds are debt instruments with their own interest-rate, credit and maturity characteristics.

Different risk profile
Speculative

Crypto & other assets

Some assets can move sharply in either direction. Large losses are possible, so risk needs to be understood first.

High volatility
Rule

Time changes the picture

Longer horizons can change how volatility is experienced, but they do not remove the possibility of loss.

Is SaveGrow Lab financial advice?

No. The site is intended for general education and calculators. A personal recommendation should take your own circumstances into account.

Are the calculators exact?

No. They are planning tools. Interest calculations use simplified assumptions and real rates, fees and returns can differ.

Can I use the tools on mobile?

Yes. The layout, calculators and article widgets are designed to work on smaller screens.

Do I need an account?

No. The current site runs locally in the browser and does not require sign-in.
