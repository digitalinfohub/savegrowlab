Do you answer personal financial questions?

We try to point readers towards the right official source (MoneyHelper, the FCA, HMRC) and the relevant guide on this site, but we cannot give personalised advice — we are educators, not regulated advisers.

How quickly will you fix a reported error?

Significant maths or factual errors are verified and corrected as soon as possible, usually within a week of confirmation, and the correction is logged.

Can I request removal of content?

Yes. Send a privacy or content request through the form or by email. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best also sent to Google via the links in our Privacy Policy.

Will my email be added to a mailing list?

No. This site has no newsletter and no CRM. Your email is used only to reply to your message, exactly as if you had written to us directly.
