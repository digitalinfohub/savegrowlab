Subscription audit

What are your subscriptions costing you?

Streaming, music, cloud storage, gym, apps, boxes. Each one is small and none of them feel optional until you add them up. List them once and see the annual figure — that is the number that changes behaviour.

The guide behind the tool

The quietest line in your budget

Subscriptions are designed to be invisible. They renew on a date you did not choose, at a price that rises by email, for a service you stopped using two months ago. Three £10 services is £360 a year — enough to fund a small emergency fund or clear a chunk of a card.

The audit is not about cancelling everything. It is about making the cost visible once, then deciding on purpose. Most people find one or two they had forgotten entirely.

How to run the audit properly

  1. Open your bank app and search for repeat payments over the last 60 days.
  2. Include annual plans — check your email for renewal receipts.
  3. Add every one to the calculator, including the “free” trial that became paid.
  4. Mark each as keep, pause or cancel. Do not decide later; later never comes.
  5. Cancel the yes-to-cancel ones today, then send the monthly amount to your goal.
Trial trap: set a phone reminder for two days before a trial ends. If you forget, the annual price lands and refunds are not guaranteed.

Household and shared plans

Family plans are often cheaper than two individual plans, and mobile contracts bundle services you may already be paying for separately. Check what your broadband, phone and bank already include before adding another service. Some current accounts include travel insurance or breakdown cover — that is a subscription you are already paying for through a fee.

Once the audit is done, put the saving somewhere visible. The savings calculator turns the annual figure into a goal, and the full audit guide has a printable checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the gym?

Yes. Anything recurring counts, including memberships, boxes and app subscriptions.

What about annual car insurance?

Insurance is essential rather than optional, but include it so the yearly total is honest.

How often should I audit?

Every three months for five minutes, and always after a free trial.

Can I cancel mid-contract?

Not always. Check the terms — some services charge a cancellation fee. Cancel at the renewal date instead.
