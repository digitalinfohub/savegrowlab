About SaveGrow Lab

Money tools that respect your time and privacy.

SaveGrow Lab is an independent UK personal-finance education site. We build free calculators that run in your browser and write plain-English guides about saving, budgeting and early-stage investing.

✓ Independent✓ No account required✓ Education only

One clear answer per question

Most people do not need another finance app. They need a quick, honest answer to a specific question: how much do I need to save for a £1,500 trip? Is my emergency fund big enough? What does 5% actually do over twenty years? SaveGrow Lab exists to answer those in seconds, show the assumptions, and point to the guide that explains what to do next.

How the guides are made

Every guide starts with primary sources — GOV.UK, HMRC, the Financial Conduct Authority, the Money and Pensions Service and MoneyHelper — is written in plain English, then checked for accuracy and for consistency with the calculators. Guides are dated and reviewed at least twice a year. If you find an error, email services@digitalinfohub.net and we will correct it and note the change.

We are not a regulated financial adviser and we do not provide personalised advice. We do not accept payment to alter editorial content, we do not publish sponsored posts, and we do not invent testimonials or statistics.

How the site is funded

SaveGrow Lab is free because it displays advertising. Adverts are served by Adsterra, an independent advertising network; we are also reviewing other ad partners and will update our privacy policy before enabling them. Advertising never influences what the guides recommend or how the calculators work. We do not sell user data — there is no user database to sell.

Privacy by architecture

No accounts, no sign-up forms, no first-party analytics. Calculator inputs stay in your browser. Your saved plan, streak and quiz score are kept in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember them, and you can clear them at any time. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Corrections and contact

Email services@digitalinfohub.net or use the contact form. We aim to reply within two to three working days.
