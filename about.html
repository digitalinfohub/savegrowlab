About SaveGrow Lab

Money tools that respect your time and privacy.

SaveGrow Lab is an independent personal-finance education site for UK savers. We build free calculators that run entirely in your browser and write plain-English guides about saving, budgeting and early-stage investing — no accounts, no data collection, no jargon.

Our mission: one clear answer per question

Most people do not need another financial app or a newsletter. They need quick, honest answers to specific questions: “How much a month do I need to save for a £1,500 trip?” “Is my emergency fund big enough?” “What does compound interest actually do over 20 years?”

SaveGrow Lab exists to answer those questions in seconds. Every tool on this site gives you a number, shows the assumptions behind it, and links to a guide that explains what to do next. If a calculation cannot be explained simply, we rewrite it until it can.

Who writes and builds this site

SaveGrow Lab is written and maintained by the Digital Infohub editorial team — a small group of writers and web developers with backgrounds in personal-finance content production. The site is published for a UK audience, so figures use pounds and sterling, and concepts specific to the UK (ISAs, FSCS protection, Personal Allowances) are covered using official sources such as HMRC, the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), the Money and Pensions Service (MaPS) and MoneyHelper.

We are not a regulated financial adviser and we never pretend to be. Nothing on this site is a personal recommendation, and we do not sell your details, broker products, or take commissions for featuring a provider.

How our editorial process works

  1. Start from primary sources. Every guide begins with official regulators and government publications, not press releases or competitor blogs.
  2. Write in plain English. Drafts are edited so a first-time saver can follow them without prior knowledge.
  3. Check the maths. Worked examples are verified against our own calculators so the article and the tool always agree.
  4. Date and review. Each guide carries a publication date; material pages are reviewed at least twice a year, and tax-related figures are re-checked whenever budgets change.
  5. Correct openly. Spot an error? Email us and we will fix significant mistakes promptly and log the correction.

Advertising revenue from Google AdSense keeps the site free, but it never influences what our guides recommend. We do not accept payment to alter content, publish sponsored posts, or fabricate testimonials, case studies or statistics.

What our tools do — and do not — do

Important: Our calculators are planning aids built on simplified, transparent assumptions (constant rates, regular contributions, month-end timing). Real-world rates change, fees reduce returns, investments can fall as well as rise, and tax depends on your circumstances. For advice tailored to you, consult a qualified financial adviser; to compare accounts or understand protections such as the Financial Services Compensation Scheme, use the official sources linked throughout our guides.

Independence and funding

Running a free, ad-supported site costs little but takes consistency. Because we hold no user data and require no account, our only incentive is usefulness: readers who trust the numbers come back, and that is exactly the model we want. If a tool cannot be both accurate and simple, we would rather not publish it.
