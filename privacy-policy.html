Privacy Policy

Short version: SaveGrow Lab has no accounts and collects no personal or financial data of its own. Every calculator runs in your browser. Your saved plan, streak and quiz score are stored only in your browser’s local storage. Advertising is served by Adsterra, which uses its own cookies and technologies, and we are reviewing other ad partners.

1. Who we are

SaveGrow Lab is an independent personal-finance education website at savegrowlab.co.uk, operated by the Digital Infohub editorial team. For UK data protection purposes we act as a controller for the limited processing described here. You can contact us at services@digitalinfohub.net.

2. What we do not collect

  • No financial data. Balances, targets, incomes, rates and every other figure you enter stay on your device. They are processed in memory by JavaScript and never transmitted to us.
  • No accounts or registration. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No newsletter lists. We only see your email address if you choose to write to us.
  • No selling or profiling of your data. We hold no user database to sell, rent or share.

3. What is stored on your device

To make the site useful across visits, we use your browser’s local storage (not first-party tracking cookies) for a small amount of functional data:

  • Saved plan — the label, target, timeline and monthly amount you ask Penny to save.
  • Saving streak — the dates you visited and marked a money minute.
  • Money health score — your most recent quiz result.
  • Interface preferences — such as whether a prompt has been dismissed in this session (session storage).

This information never leaves your device, is not linked to your identity, and can be deleted at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings. It is not accessible to us.

4. Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / GitHub B.V.). Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page and timestamp for security and availability. We do not control, mine or sell those logs. Our legal basis for any such limited processing is legitimate interest in running a secure website.

5. Advertising: Adsterra

SaveGrow Lab is free because it displays advertising. Adverts are served by Adsterra, an independent advertising network. Adsterra and its partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers and approximate location (derived from IP address) to serve, measure and — where permitted — personalise adverts. This processing is governed by Adsterra’s own privacy policy, not ours.

We may also work with other advertising partners in future. We are reviewing other networks now; when a new partner is enabled, this policy will be updated first to name it and link to its privacy information.

  • Adsterra privacy policy
  • Your Online Choices — manage interest-based advertising in Europe
  • About Ads — US opt-out choices

We receive only aggregated, non-identifying reporting about ad performance, such as impression and revenue totals. We do not receive your name, email address or financial information from Adsterra.

7. Analytics and third parties

We run no first-party analytics and load no external fonts or UI frameworks. Our guides link out to official sources such as HMRC, the FCA, MoneyHelper, StepChange and the FSCS using ordinary links; their trackers are not embedded on our pages. Embedding a third-party video or widget in future would require updating this policy first.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). Because we hold essentially no personal data, most requests will be answered by explaining that we hold nothing to disclose. Email services@digitalinfohub.net and we will respond within one month.

9. Children

The site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this page whenever our practices change — including when a new advertising partner is added. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the current version.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: services@digitalinfohub.net. See also our terms and conditions.
