1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy applies to savegrowlab.co.uk ("SaveGrow Lab", "we", "us"), an independent personal-finance education website operated by the Digital Infohub editorial team. It explains what information is processed when you use our calculators, read our guides, or contact us, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

We process any limited data described here as a "controller" under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Our guiding principle is data minimisation: we deliberately architect the site so that there is almost nothing to collect.

2. The data we do NOT collect

  • No financial data. Balances, targets, incomes, interest rates and every other figure you enter into a calculator stay on your device. They are processed in memory by JavaScript and are never transmitted to us.
  • No accounts or registration. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No newsletter or marketing lists. We do not ask for your email address unless you choose to write to us.
  • No first-party cookies, local storage or tracking pixels. The site functions identically whether or not third-party cookies are allowed.
  • No profiling or sale of data. We have no user database to sell, rent or share.

3. Data processed when you visit

Although we personally collect nothing, delivering a web page over the internet necessarily involves some technical processing by infrastructure providers:

  • Standard server log data. Our static host may record technical request information such as IP address (often truncated), browser type, operating system, referring URL, requested page and timestamp. This exists for security, abuse prevention and basic operational diagnostics, not for building user profiles.
  • Messages you send us. If you use the contact form or email us, the content you write (including your name and email if you provide them) is processed solely to respond to your enquiry. The contact form composes an email through your own mail client; we operate no form backend and store no submissions in a CRM.
  • Advertising interactions. Google AdSense processes certain information about ads shown to you — see sections 5 and 6.

4. How calculator inputs are handled

All six calculators (savings goal, interest & growth, monthly budget, trip budget, £5 basket estimate and emergency fund checker) are implemented as client-side JavaScript. When you press "Calculate", the arithmetic happens inside your browser tab. Nothing is submitted, queued, cached on a server, or added to your browsing history as form data. Closing the tab discards everything. This is a deliberate design choice: a money-planning tool should not need to know who you are.

6. Advertising: Google AdSense

To keep SaveGrow Lab free, we display advertisements served by Google AdSense, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited ("Google"). Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and — where you permit it — personalise ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. Behavioural advertising is regulated under PECR in the UK, and Google participates in self-regulatory programmes for behavioural advertising.

Relevant third-party disclosures and controls:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings — review, delete or turn off personalised advertising
  • About Ads choices page (US-based opt-out)
  • YourOnlineChoices (European interactive digital advertising alliance opt-out)

Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) rather than by ours. We receive only aggregated, non-personally-identifiable reporting about ad performance (for example impression and revenue totals).

7. Analytics and third-party services

  • Own analytics: none at present. Any future addition would require updating this policy and, where required by law, asking for consent first.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense (described above).
  • Fonts and libraries: the site uses system fonts and no external UI frameworks, so no additional third parties are loaded when you browse.
  • Embedded content: our guides link out to official sources (HMRC, FCA, MoneyHelper, MaPS, FSCS) using ordinary links. We do not embed their trackers or videos.

8. Hosting, servers and technical logs

SaveGrow Lab is hosted on static infrastructure (GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. / GitHub B.V.). Like most web servers, the host may keep standard technical access logs for security and operational purposes. We do not control, sell or mine those logs; the legal basis for any such limited processing is legitimate interest in operating a secure, available website.

9. Sharing, international transfers and disclosure to law

We do not sell your data and hold essentially none to share. Exceptions, kept narrow:

  • Service providers: our hosting provider and Google (advertising) process the limited technical data described above under their own terms.
  • Legal obligation: if compelled by a valid legal process (for example a court order), a provider may be required to disclose log data. We would notify you where legally permitted.
  • Business transfer: if the site were acquired, users would be told before any data (as it exists) changed hands under a different policy.

Some providers process data outside the UK, e.g. in the United States. Where that happens, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as applied by those providers.

10. Retention

We retain no user records. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and resolve it, then deleted from our mailbox. Host access-log retention is governed by the hosting provider's policies.

11. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of personal data we hold about you; to restrict or object to processing; and to data portability. Because we hold no personal data of our own, these requests typically resolve to "there is nothing to return" — but if you emailed us something, we will confirm what we hold and delete it on request.

  • Contact us: email services@digitalinfohub.net or use the contact page. We respond within one month, as required by law.
  • Google-held advertising data: exercise access, deletion and personalisation controls directly via Google Ads Settings and Google's privacy tools.
  • Complain: you can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), ico.org.uk, at any time — though we would appreciate the chance to fix things first.

12. Children's privacy

SaveGrow Lab is a general-audience educational site and is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age threshold in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because advertising partners may process data differently for minors, we recommend parental guidance for younger visitors.

13. Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS with encryption in transit. The strongest security feature is architectural: since calculator data never leaves your device, there is no user-data store to breach. We keep the site's code minimal and review it when dependencies change.

14. Changes to this policy

If the site's functionality changes — for example if we add analytics, a comments system, or a consent manager — we will update this policy before the change goes live, and material updates will be dated at the top of this page. The date shown ("Last updated") reflects the current version.

15. How to contact us

Data protection enquiries, correction or deletion requests regarding emails you have sent us, and any privacy questions: services@digitalinfohub.net. See our Contact page for response times and what to include.
