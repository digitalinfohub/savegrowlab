Can you afford all of your goals?

Real life does not have one savings goal. Holidays, repairs, deposits and emergencies compete for the same pay. Add up to four goals and see exactly what they cost together — and which one to stretch.

The guide behind the tool

Which goal should get the money first?

Not the most exciting one. A sensible funding order is: a starter emergency buffer, then any employer pension match, then high-rate debt, then goals by the cost of missing them. A rental deposit has a harder deadline than a holiday, and a broken boiler has no deadline at all — it just happens.

The comparison tool does not decide for you. It shows the monthly cost of each goal so the decision is made with a number instead of a feeling.

When the total is over budget

Stretch deadlines before cutting targets. A £1,500 holiday over 10 months is £150 a month; over 15 months it is £100. Three goals that each gain a few months often fit inside a budget that none of them fit into individually. The tool tells you the exact overshoot so you know how much stretching is needed.

Do not fund everything at once. Two goals funded properly beat four funded at 40%. Pause the least urgent, finish one, then start the next. Momentum is a real financial asset.

Keep the pots separate

One account called “savings” with £4,000 in it tells you nothing about whether the holiday is funded. Use named pots — many UK apps support them — or separate easy-access accounts. The sinking funds guide explains how to split regular costs into monthly amounts so they stop arriving as emergencies.

If the maths says one goal is unrealistic, that is useful information, not a failure. The budget planner may find more room, and the saving guide covers the behavioural side.

Frequently asked questions

How many goals can I compare?

Four at a time. Combine small goals into one “miscellaneous” pot if you need to go further.

Should interest be included?

Only for goals more than about 18 months away, and only at a rate you can actually get. Otherwise leave it at zero.

What if one goal has no deadline?

Give it a generous one — 24 or 36 months — so the monthly cost is honest but not crushing.

Does it save my goals?

No. This worksheet runs in memory only. Save the key numbers with Penny if you want them kept on this device.
